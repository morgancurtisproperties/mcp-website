Who we buy from

Most of the homes we buy come from landlords who are ready to step back. Some want a straightforward sale without paying an agent. Others have a portfolio and want to sell it in a way that works around their lending and each specific property in their portfolio.

We're currently working with one portfolio landlord to buy six of his properties, structured around what works for him rather than a standard sale. One of the key things we structured was to avoid early repayment charges on his mortgage, which would unnecessarily eat into his profits. The transaction works equally well, if not better, structured this way for both parties.

What to expect

It's rarely just about the money

When someone sells a property there's usually more to it than the price: time frames, amounts, what happens next. I find out exactly what you need from the sale and structure the purchase around it.

One recent seller's priority was that her friend, who was already living there, could keep renting the house. Once that was clear, we structured a deal that delivered that outcome and worked commercially for both sides.

The best deals are the ones where you take the time to find out what's driving the decision. It's surprising, but it isn't always money that drives decisions in property transactions.

I love a win-win deal where everyone is happy with the result. In this case, all three parties were happy: the tenant, the vendor, and me as the buyer and now the landlord.

Portfolio landlords

Thinking about selling your rental property or your portfolio?

We're portfolio landlords ourselves, so we know the headaches: leaking roofs, fire alarms going off, boilers breaking. At some point the hassle stops being worth it, and the idea of money in the bank and a quieter life starts to look very appealing.

Buying in stages

Purchases can be staggered over time in a way that suits you. We can time completion so that it works with when your fixed rate loans expire.

Structured around you

Not every sale needs to be one transaction on one date. We'll explore the different ways we could buy your property and talk you through each option, so we find the best way forward for you.

An experienced team

We work with solicitors and mortgage brokers who are experienced in both buying and selling portfolios.

If that sounds familiar, we'd love to speak to you, even if it's just to have a grumble about being a landlord at the moment.
